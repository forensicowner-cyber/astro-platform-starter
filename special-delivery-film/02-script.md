# 02 — VISUAL SCRIPT  ("SPECIAL DELIVERY" · SILENT FILM)

**Runtime target:** 5:00 · **Format:** silent visual comedy — **NO narrator, NO dialogue.**
Story is told through action, expressions, music, and sound effects. The only on-screen words
are *in-world*: the **"UNDELIVERABLE"** stamp and the **medal engraving**.

> This is a beat sheet describing what the audience SEES and HEARS (music/SFX). Use it alongside
> `03-shot-list.md` (the actual Seedance prompts) and `05-audio-plan.md` (music + SFX).

---

## ACT 1 — SETUP  (0:00 – 1:15)

**0:00–0:08 · Cold open.** A tiny glowing planet drifts beside a giant ringed gas planet. Tiny
teal city-lights twinkle. *(Music M1: whimsical music-box wonder.)*

**0:08–0:16 · The little world.** Push into a gleaming teal crystal city. Tiny aliens scurry
happily, raising festive banners. Pure joy and anticipation.

**0:16–0:24 · The ceremony.** Elder Pim raises a glowing staff before a cheering crowd. They are
clearly celebrating something huge — a visitor is coming. *(Read entirely through body language.)*

**0:24–0:32 · Hope.** The crowd looks up; a faint colossal shadow forms in the clouds. Awe on
every tiny face. *(Music swells, a low rumble enters.)*

**0:32–0:48 · Hard cut — the courier.** A messy cockpit. A bored, slouching courier chews gum,
sips a slushie, scrolls his device. A red alert blinks; he ignores it, waves it off. *(Tone
flips from magical to lazy/comedic — music drops to a slacker groove.)*

**0:48–0:56 · The assignment.** Dashboard screen: a parcel icon + "DELIVERY 4012 — DEST:
PEBBLE-9". He doesn't really look.

**0:56–1:15 · Descent.** The chunky orange ship turns toward the little planet. From the city,
a giant ship silhouette eclipses the sun; the aliens kneel in reverence. *(Music M2 begins —
grand but uneasy.)*

---

## ACT 2 — ESCALATION  (1:15 – 3:30)

**1:15–1:32 · The landing (apocalypse #1).** A moon-sized boot-shaped landing gear descends and
slams down. Earthquake. Towers crack. The aliens still cheer — they think it's a blessing.

**1:32–1:40 · Pim rejoices.** The elder throws his arms up in joy, oblivious to the cracks
spreading around him. *(Bittersweet: their happiest moment = their doom.)*

**1:40–1:58 · The courier steps out.** He clomps down the ramp onto a rock the size of a
basketball, scanner + parcel + slushie in hand, squints for an address, totally unimpressed.

**1:58–2:24 · The search (apocalypse #2).** Each footstep flattens a district (we see it from
the tiny streets — terror; from his eye-level — nothing). He leans on a "rock" — the capital
dome — and shatters it. A slushie drop becomes a tidal wave flooding a plaza. He notices none of it.

**2:24–2:57 · The last stand (apocalypse #3).** The aliens launch their entire glowing fleet at
his boot. The volley bursts like harmless glitter. He pauses... and scratches the "itch,"
wiping out the fleet without looking. *(M3: brave little theme that comically fizzles into a
sad sting.)*

**2:57–3:15 · Pim's plea.** Elder Pim climbs the last spire and waves his staff at the giant
face above, straining, tears in his eyes. The courier tilts his head — did he hear something? —
glances down, sees only dust, shrugs.

**3:15–3:30 · Giving up.** His device flashes "DELIVERY WINDOW CLOSING." He rolls his eyes and
switches off the scanner. Nobody home, he decides.

---

## ACT 3 — TWIST  (3:30 – 5:00)

**3:30–3:39 · The verdict.** Scanner: **"RECIPIENT NOT FOUND."** His gloved hand slams a red
**"UNDELIVERABLE"** stamp on the parcel. *(First on-screen words.)*

**3:39–3:47 · Still hoping.** The half-crushed city; a few survivors still gaze up, hopeful.
*(Music M4: lone piano enters.)*

**3:47–4:11 · The toss (final apocalypse).** He turns to go and casually tosses the heavy parcel
over his shoulder. From the tiny streets: a brown mountain falls from the heavens. **BOOM.** Dust.
Then sudden, total silence.

**4:11–4:36 · Liftoff.** He clomps back up the ramp, never looking back. The ship roars off; the
exhaust scorches the last fragments to ash. Pull back: the once-glittering planet is now a gray,
smoking crater. The ship shrinks to a dot.

**4:36–4:53 · THE REVEAL.** Slow push on the cracked-open parcel in the rubble: a gleaming golden
**medal**. The camera reads the engraving:
> **"GALACTIC HONOR — TO THE PEOPLE OF PEBBLE-9 — MOST PEACEFUL CIVILIZATION IN THE GALAXY."**
*(M5: the happy opening melody, now slow and heartbreaking.)*

**4:53–5:00 · Final image.** One tiny soot-covered survivor crawls to the giant medal and rests
a hand on it, looking up at the empty sky. Pull back into darkness. **SMASH TO BLACK. TITLE
CARD: "SPECIAL DELIVERY."**

---

## Why silent works here
- The comedy is **dramatic irony** — we see what he doesn't. That needs no words.
- The tragedy lands harder in silence: the crushed world never gets to be heard, which is
  literally the film's point ("the kindest world no one ever noticed").
- Fewer words = easier global reach on YouTube and no lip-sync headaches.
