# 02 — FULL SCRIPT  ("SPECIAL DELIVERY")

**Runtime target:** 5:00 · **Format:** narrated comedy short ·
**VO = Narrator (N)** · Character lines are short and mostly comedic/garbled.

> Timecodes are approximate. Narration word counts are tuned for a calm pace (~130 wpm).
> Total narration ≈ 620 words ≈ 4:45 of speech with pauses for visual gags.

---

## ACT 1 — SETUP  (0:00 – 1:15)

### 0:00 – 0:12 · COLD OPEN — space
Wide, peaceful shot: a glowing little planet (Pebble-9) drifts beside a giant ringed
gas planet. Tiny city-lights twinkle on its surface.

**N:** "In the quietest corner of the galaxy, there was a planet no bigger than a pebble.
And on it lived the smallest, kindest people you would never, ever notice."

### 0:12 – 0:30 · The Zibbit city
Macro push-in to the gleaming crystal city. Tiny teal Zibbits scurry happily.
Banners are being raised. ELDER PIM lifts his glowing staff to a cheering crowd.

**N:** "Today was the most important day in Zibbit history. The sky had spoken — a great
visitor was coming. They had been chosen."

**ELDER PIM:** *(tiny, joyful voice)* "He comes! The Sky Titan comes to bless us!"

### 0:30 – 0:55 · Meet RIGGS
Hard cut to a messy spaceship cockpit. RIGGS slouches in his seat, chewing gum,
sipping a slushie, scrolling his comm-device. A dashboard alert blinks, ignored.

**N:** "Meanwhile, several million miles away, the galaxy's greatest courier was hard at work."

**RIGGS:** *(bored, mouth full)* "Uh-huh... yeah... no, I'm totally working right now."

A robotic dashboard voice pings.
**SHIP AI:** "Delivery 4-0-1-2. One parcel. Destination: Pebble-9."
**RIGGS:** *(groans)* "Ugh. A pebble. Great."

### 0:55 – 1:15 · The descent begins
Riggs flicks a switch without looking. The ship turns toward the little planet.
On Pebble-9, the Zibbits gasp in awe as a colossal shape blots out their sky.

**N:** "He did not read the label. He never read the label."

---

## ACT 2 — ESCALATION  (1:15 – 3:30)

### 1:15 – 1:35 · The landing (apocalypse #1)
Riggs' enormous boot-shaped landing gear lowers. From the Zibbit POV: a moon-sized
foot descends from the clouds. It touches down — **EARTHQUAKE.** Crystal towers
shudder. Zibbits cheer anyway, mistaking it for a blessing.

**N:** "To them, it was the touch of a god. To him, it was a Tuesday."

**ELDER PIM:** *(awed)* "He has arrived! Rejoice!"

### 1:35 – 2:00 · Riggs steps out
Riggs clomps down the ramp, scanner in one hand, parcel under his arm, slushie in the other.
He squints at the tiny rock.

**RIGGS:** "Okay... where's the address...?"

He takes a step. His boot crushes an entire suburb of the crystal city. He doesn't notice.
The Zibbits below scatter and scream.

**N:** "He was looking for a doorbell. They were looking for a miracle. Neither would find one."

### 2:00 – 2:25 · The search (apocalypse #2)
Riggs wanders, scanning. Each footstep flattens a district. He leans on a "small rock"
— it's the Zibbit capital dome; it shatters under his glove.

**RIGGS:** *(annoyed)* "Place is a dump. No house numbers, nothing."

He sips his slushie. A drop falls — to the Zibbits it's a tidal wave that floods a plaza.

### 2:25 – 2:55 · The Zibbits fight back
Brave Zibbits launch their entire defense fleet — tiny glowing airships — at Riggs' boot.
The volley lands. Riggs pauses... and scratches his ankle.

**RIGGS:** "Huh. Mosquitoes? In space?"

**N:** "Their bravest pilots flew their final mission. He thought it was an itch."

### 2:55 – 3:30 · Pim's plea
ELDER PIM climbs to the highest surviving spire and waves his glowing staff at the sky,
shouting up at the giant face in the clouds.

**ELDER PIM:** "Great one! We are HERE! We welcome you! Please — see us!"

Riggs, far above, hears a faint squeak. He glances down, sees nothing but dust, shrugs,
and checks his comm again. The dashboard pings: "Delivery window closing."

**RIGGS:** "Yeah, yeah. Nobody's even home."

---

## ACT 3 — TWIST  (3:30 – 5:00)

### 3:30 – 3:55 · Giving up
Riggs scans one last time. The scanner blinks **"RECIPIENT NOT FOUND."** He sighs,
pulls out a stamp, and slaps **"UNDELIVERABLE"** across the parcel.

**RIGGS:** "Not my problem. Marking it 'nobody home.'"

**N:** "And so, the galaxy's greatest courier prepared to leave the way he came."

### 3:55 – 4:20 · The toss (apocalypse #final)
Riggs casually tosses the heavy parcel over his shoulder as he turns to go. From the
Zibbit POV: a brown mountain falls from the heavens onto the last standing district.
**BOOM.** Dust. Silence.

### 4:20 – 4:40 · Liftoff
Riggs clomps back up the ramp, already on his comm. His ship roars, lifts off — the
backwash scorches what little remains. The little planet, once glittering, is now a
gray, smoking crater.

**RIGGS:** *(fading)* "...nah, total ghost town. Tell 'em recipient's not found."

### 4:40 – 4:58 · THE REVEAL
Slow push on the discarded parcel, lying cracked open in the rubble. Inside: a small,
gleaming golden **medal**. The camera reads the engraving:

> **"GALACTIC HONOR — TO THE PEOPLE OF PEBBLE-9
> THE MOST PEACEFUL CIVILIZATION IN THE GALAXY."**

**N:** "The package was an award. The little planet had won the galaxy's highest honor —
for being the kindest world ever found."

### 4:58 – 5:00 · Final image
One tiny, soot-covered Zibbit survivor crawls from the dust, reaches the giant medal,
and rests a small hand on it as Riggs' ship shrinks to a dot in the stars.

**N:** *(soft)* "They were finally seen... by no one at all."

**SMASH TO BLACK. TITLE CARD: "SPECIAL DELIVERY".**
