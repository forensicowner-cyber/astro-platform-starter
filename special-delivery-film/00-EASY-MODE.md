## The core trick: the giant is NEVER fully seen

The whole film is shot **from the tiny world's point of view.** The courier (the "giant")
only ever appears as:
- a **shadow** sweeping over the city,
- a colossal **orange-and-white boot**,
- a giant **white-gloved fingertip**,
- a falling **brown parcel**,
- a distant **dark ship silhouette** with glowing engines,
- a **slushie droplet**, a **scanner light beam**.

**We never see his face or body clearly.** That single decision removes the hardest job in
AI video — keeping a character's face the same across shots — because there's no face to keep.

### Why this is so much easier
| Hard thing (old plan) | Easy mode fix |
|---|---|
| Keep Riggs' face identical in 20 shots | No face shown — ever |
| Keep one hero alien consistent | Use crowds + one-off elders; no repeated face |
| Keep the city identical | Show different districts — variety looks like a big city |
| Match a boot/hand exactly | Slight differences read as natural; nobody notices |
| 8–10 reference images | **Just 1 needed** (the city look) |

## Length flexibility
- Core E-01 → E-25 ≈ **3:30–4:00** of footage. With holds, the title card, and 2–3 extra
  shots (E-26 → E-30) you'll comfortably hit **~5:00**.
- A tight **3:30 cut is also great** for AI shorts — shorter often means higher watch-through.

## Consistency cheat-sheet (read this!)
1. **Never show the giant's face/body** — only boot, glove, shadow, parcel, ship silhouette.
2. Describe the giant's parts the same way each time: *orange-and-white sci-fi boot*,
   *white-gloved fingertip*, *dark ship silhouette with blue engines*. Small drift is invisible.
3. Show **different city districts** each shot — variety reads as one big city, not as errors.
4. Use **crowds**, not a repeated hero face (the elder and survivor each appear once, so they
   never need to match anything).
5. Keep the **style line identical** in every prompt, and attach **R-WORLD** to city shots.
6. Add **film grain + vignette** in editing — it glues AI clips together and hides differences.
