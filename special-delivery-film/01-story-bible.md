# 01 — STORY BIBLE

## Logline

A lazy, distracted intergalactic courier is sent to deliver a single package to a
tiny planet — never realizing the planet is home to a microscopic civilization that
worships his arrival as a god... right up until he accidentally flattens them while
looking for the doorbell.

## Title

**SPECIAL DELIVERY** *(working title)*
Alt titles: "Recipient Not Found", "Pebble-9", "The Galaxy's Best Courier"

## Tone & genre

- **Genre:** Sci-fi comedy with a dark/ironic twist.
- **Tone:** Light and funny for 80% of the runtime, then a sudden bittersweet gut-punch
  in the final 30 seconds (this is the *Johnny Express* "feel").
- **Comedy engine:** Dramatic irony — the audience sees the tiny world; the giant never does.
  Every "small" action by the courier is an apocalypse for the little people.

## The Twist (keep secret until the end)

The package the courier carries is a **galactic award**: a "Most Peaceful Civilization
in the Galaxy" medal, addressed to the people of Pebble-9. By the time he gives up
searching and tosses the undelivered package away, he has already crushed their entire
world. The peaceful little planet wins its greatest honor on the same day it is wiped
out — and the courier flies off without ever knowing they existed.

## CHARACTERS

### RIGGS — the courier (protagonist)
- **Role:** Delivery driver for "WARP EXPRESS", the galaxy's cheapest shipping company.
- **Look:** Bulky humanoid in a worn **orange jumpsuit**, scuffed white space-boots,
  oversized bubble helmet with a cracked visor, a company cap, perpetual gum-chewing.
  Slightly overweight, slouched posture, bored half-lidded eyes.
- **Personality:** Lazy, distracted, always on his comm-device, does the bare minimum,
  not evil — just utterly oblivious and self-absorbed.
- **Signature props:** A glowing handheld scanner, a single brown cardboard-style
  space-parcel with a red "FRAGILE" stamp, a slushie drink he keeps sipping.
- **Consistency anchors (must appear every shot):** orange jumpsuit, cracked bubble
  helmet, WARP EXPRESS logo on chest, the brown parcel.

### THE ZIBBITS — the tiny civilization
- **Look:** Tiny (2 mm tall) round, glowing blue-green beings with big eyes, four arms,
  living in a gleaming miniature **crystal city** on a pebble-sized planet.
- **Tech level:** Advanced and peaceful — clean energy spires, tiny airships, a domed capital.
- **Behavior:** Hopeful, ceremonial. They detect Riggs' ship and believe a benevolent
  "Sky Titan" is descending to bless them.

### ELDER PIM — the Zibbit leader
- **Look:** Slightly taller Zibbit with a glowing ceremonial staff and a flowing robe of light.
- **Role:** Leads the welcome ceremony, narrates the Zibbits' hope, reacts to the disaster.
  The emotional anchor of the little world.

### THE NARRATOR (voiceover)
- Warm, wry, storybook documentary voice (think nature-documentary-meets-fairy-tale).
- Speaks ABOUT the events, heightening the irony. Never breaks to comedy — plays it straight,
  which makes it funnier and sadder.

## WORLD / SETTINGS

1. **Riggs' delivery ship** — cramped, messy cockpit; fast-food wrappers, blinking
   dashboard, a cracked windshield view of stars.
2. **Deep space / approach** — Pebble-9 as a glowing speck near a big ringed gas giant.
3. **Pebble-9 surface** — from the Zibbit POV: a vast crystal metropolis, spires, airships,
   a grand welcome plaza. From Riggs' POV: a tiny dusty rock the size of a basketball.
4. **The crater finale** — the flattened, smoking remains; one survivor; a single medal.

## VISUAL STYLE (lock this for every prompt)

- **Render:** Polished **Pixar/DreamWorks-style 3D animation**, cinematic lighting,
  shallow depth of field, soft global illumination.
- **Palette:** Warm oranges for Riggs/ship interiors; cool glowing **cyan-teal** for the
  Zibbit world (strong contrast = readable scale difference).
- **Camera:** Mix of (a) huge wide shots for scale, (b) tiny ground-level macro shots in
  the Zibbit city, (c) comedic close-ups on Riggs' bored face.
- **Aspect ratio:** **16:9**, cinematic. Target **1080p**.
- **Recurring visual gag:** Cut between "epic for them / nothing for him" — e.g. a
  city-leveling earthquake (their POV) = Riggs just shifting his weight (his POV).
