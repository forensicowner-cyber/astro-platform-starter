# NOVA — Lightspeed Universe Vlog (Series Production Package)

A scientifically-accurate space-exploration **vlog series** starring **Nova**, a recurring
AI-avatar host who sets out to travel the **entire universe at the speed of light**.

## The premise in one paragraph
In the year **2100**, humanity cracked biological immortality — humans became effectively
**invincible and stopped aging**. Decades later, one explorer, **Nova**, boards a light-speed
ship (the **Aurora**) to do what no mortal ever could: **see the whole universe with her own
eyes** and vlog the journey back to Earth. Because she never ages, her look never changes —
which is also how we keep the avatar perfectly consistent across every episode.

## Two hard rules for every episode
1. **REAL PHYSICS ONLY.** Every size, distance, temperature, speed, and light-travel time shown
   must be the actual real-world value. Numbers appear as on-screen data cards. (See the science
   sheet in `01-series-bible.md`.)
2. **Light-speed = real travel times.** Since the Aurora moves at light speed, the time to reach
   anything equals its real light-distance (Moon in ~1.3 s, the Sun in ~8 min, etc.). This keeps
   the fiction and the facts in sync.

## How to use this package (in order)
| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `01-series-bible.md` | Lore, Nova's character, format, Season 1 roadmap, the science rules + data sheet |
| `02-avatar-consistency-kit.md` | Reference-image prompts + the method to keep Nova identical (face + voice) |

## Workflow
```
1. Build Nova's reference images + lock her AI voice   (file 02)  ← do once
2. Generate Episode 1 clips in Seedance 2              (file 03)
3. Record Nova's voice (consistent AI voice) per script(file 03)
4. Edit: clips + voice + music + on-screen data cards  (file 03)
5. Export 1080p (or 9:16 for Shorts) + publish         (file 03)
```

## Tools
- **Seedance 2.0** — the video clips (POV space + cockpit selfie shots).
- **Image generator** — Nova's reference frames (Midjourney / Seedance image mode / any).
- **AI voice** — one consistent voice for Nova (e.g., ElevenLabs). Her voice = her identity.
- **Editor** — CapCut or DaVinci Resolve. **Data-card overlays** are key to the "real physics" feel.
- **Music** — royalty-free (YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay, Uppbeat).

> Name/vibe is easily changeable — "Nova" is the default. Rename via find-and-replace anytime.
